Connected Services

Connected services are designed to enhance your driving experience by offering convenience, safety, and performance. Leapmotor brings these benefits to life with features like remote vehicle preparation, smart navigation, and continuous monitoring, all accessible at your fingertips. Stay in control, whether you're on the road or preparing for your journey, with seamless connectivity that keeps you informed and secure.

BEFORE DRIVING - REMOTE PREPARATION & CONVENIENCE

CHARGING MANAGEMENT

Scheduled charging, charging status, state of charge (SoC) target setup, remote charging/decharging control

VEHICLE PRE-CONDITIONING

Remote battery preheating, remote AC control, remote seat heating, remote steeringwheel preheating, remote front/rear windshields preheating, remote rearview mirror. preheating, one-touch vehicle preconditioning

REMOTE ACCESS & SECURITY

Remote unlocking/locking, Bluetooth keyless entry, remote trunk door control, remote window control

NAVIGATION & TRIP PLANNING

Navigation address send to vehicle, charging route plan

WHILE DRIVING - SMART CONTROL & INFOTAINMENT

INTELLIGENT NAVIGATION & ASSISTANCE

Built-in navigation, real-time traffic info, turn-by-turn navigation on cluster, ISA - Intelligent Speed Limit Assistance

VOICE & DISPLAY CONTROLS

Voice command, user guidelines, onboard notification, time display

INFOTAINMENT & CONNECTIVITY

4G connectivity, defined 4G data for music/3rd party apps, online music, music display on cluster, 3rd party Appstore

AFTER DRIVING - MONITORING & SECURITY

VEHICLE STATUS MONITORING

State of charge (SoC) status display, range status display, doors/windows/AC status display

SECURITY & SAFETY ALERTS

Failures/errors upload to cloud, remote locating vehicle by horn honking, life detection alert to app

SOFTWARES & UPDATES

Over the air updates
