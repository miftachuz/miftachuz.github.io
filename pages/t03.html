SMALL OUTSIDE BUT LARGE INSIDE

Redefining compactness, A segment with B segment roominess.

Measuring just 3.62 meters in length the T03 is small on the outside but offers a generous interior space at the same time. Its compact dimensions make it remarkably agile and effortless to handle, slipping easily through narrow streets and tight parking spots. Inside, the space is impressively well used, among the best in its category, ensuring a comfortable ride for all occupants. Even taller passengers will appreciate the thoughtful design, with up to 950 mm of headroom in the front and 960 mm in the rear.
