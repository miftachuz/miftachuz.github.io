Green Technology for Everyone

We, Leapmotor, believe that the future of sustainable mobility should be accessible to everyone. And since the creation of our brand in 2015 ,we've taken the challenge to democratize green mobility by continuously developing products that combines cutting-edge technologies, eco-friendly innovations and affordability to redefine a green mobility for all. Our intelligent powertrain, our battery pack and management technologies are all developed in-house and was the world's first pure EV company to implement CTC (Cell-To-Chassis) technology on a large scale since 2022. This approach allows us to a competitive edge on quality and cost control, and to deliver exceptional values to our customers.
