Your Leapmotor, Always Evolving

Software updates delivered remotely to improve functionality, safety, and user experience.

An Over-The-Air (OTA) update allows a car’s software to be updated remotely, without visiting a dealership. Connected vehicles receive updates via the internet, enabling manufacturers to fix bugs, enhance security, improve driver-assistance systems, and add new features.

This technology ensures that a vehicle evolves over time, stays compliant with the latest safety and software standards, and continuously adapts to the driver’s needs. It’s similar to how smartphones receive regular updates, but for your car, keeping it safer, more efficient, and always up to date.

UPDATE YOUR CAR

A brand-new LEAP OS is here to make your car even smarter and smoother to drive!
Follow these quick steps to check, download, and install the update — it only takes a few minutes.

Check your version first

Go to Settings → System → General to see if your vehicle is up to date.

Look for the download icon

Take the time to drive your vehicle.
If an update is available, a little cloud icon will appear at the top left of your central screen.

Download the update

Tap the cloud to display a pop-up & start downloading — you can even do this while driving!

Download the update

Once the download is complete the cloud will appear in green, you’ll need to park your vehicle to start the installation.
Sit back and relax while your car applies the new LEAP OS.
